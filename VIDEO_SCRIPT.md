# Client Presentation Video Script: Dr. Maya Reynolds, PsyD
**Format:** 5-Minute Client Walkthrough (Loom / neetoRecord)  
**Speaker Tone:** Confident, warm, articulate, non-technical, studio partner presenting to Dr. Reynolds.

---

### [0:00 – 0:45] Opening & Strategic Positioning
*(Camera on you smiling, then screen shares the desktop homepage)*

"Here's your new website draft, Dr. Reynolds.

When thoughtful, high-achieving adults look for therapy in Santa Monica, they are rarely looking for generic clinical platitudes or cold medical portals. They are exhausted, overthinking, and bracing against internal pressure—yet they appear completely functional to the outside world.

Our goal with this design was to build a digital presence that feels like stepping into your Santa Monica office before they even book a session: calm, private, grounded, and deeply human. Every word, color, and spacing decision is crafted specifically around your practice scope—welcoming adult clients in-person at 123th Street 45 W, and through secure telehealth across California."

---

### [0:45 – 1:45] Design Craft, Color Palette & Typography
*(Slowly scroll slightly down the Hero section, pointing out the subtle textures and visual rhythm)*

"Let’s look at the visual atmosphere. You’ll notice immediately that this doesn’t look like an AI-generated template or a conventional clinic website.

First, the color palette:
- We grounded the design with **Evergreen Ink**, a rich, deep forest tone that gives the site gravitas, stability, and authority.
- The background is a soft **Warm Cream** accented with natural **Sand**, creating an unhurried, sunlit warmth that is gentle on tired eyes.
- We added subtle touches of **Sage** for hairline dividing lines, and an earthy **Clay** for our buttons and interactive links. That clay tone isn't loud or aggressive like typical marketing buttons—it feels rooted in coastal California earth, while meeting the highest accessibility standards for readability.

For typography, we paired a bespoke editorial serif, *Cormorant Garamond*, for your headings—with gentle italicized words like *quiet* in the headline—against a modern, highly legible sans-serif for body text. There is also a very subtle, tactile paper-grain texture across the entire page, giving it the bespoke feel of high-end stationery rather than sterile digital software."

---

### [1:45 – 2:45] Desktop Walkthrough: Hero, Hope & 'Who I Help'
*(Scroll through the Hero, into Hope, and down to the Three Focus Cards)*

"In the Hero, your visitors immediately see your dual scope: in-person sessions here in Santa Monica, and telehealth for adult clients statewide. We paired your portrait with a window-light vignette of your office, framed in an elegant architectural arch that symbolizes entering a protected space.

Right below the hero is the **Hope Section**: *'You don’t have to stay braced against your own life.'*
This speaks directly to the experience of high-functioning anxiety and overthinking. Instead of lecturing the reader, it validates what they are feeling quietly inside.

Moving down, we have the **Who I Help** section, divided into your three core areas:
1. **Anxiety & Panic**—for clients feeling chronic hypervigilance, tightness in the chest, and physical alarm.
2. **Trauma & Long-Standing Patterns**—highlighting your stabilization-first approach for both single events and childhood relational patterns.
3. **Burnout & Perfectionism**—crafted specifically for the entrepreneurs, creatives, and professionals you see every day.

Notice the section right beneath it: a statement band in deep evergreen, featuring large, rhythmic italic titles for your areas of expertise, separated by fine hairline rules. It creates a striking, elevated visual break that commands attention."

---

### [2:45 – 3:45] How You Work, Clinical Specialties & 'Our Office'
*(Scroll through How I Work, Specialties, and stop at Our Office)*

"In **How I Work**, we feature your collaborative clinical philosophy. We explain how you combine CBT, EMDR, mindfulness, and body-oriented somatic techniques. This reassures prospective clients that therapy will address not only their thoughts, but also the physiological tension held in their nervous system.

Following your four specialty tracks, we arrive at the dedicated **Our Office** section.
This was a key requirement for your practice. It highlights your quiet, uncluttered space at 123th Street 45 W in Santa Monica, flooded with natural light. We’ve featured three asymmetric editorial photos showing the comfortable seating and calm texture, accompanied by an instant *Get Directions* link and a clear reminder that California clients can also meet you via secure video.

Below this, we have your **Schedule CTA** and an interactive **FAQ Accordion** that answers all the practical questions: who you work with, your trauma approach, your office environment, and fees. Notice that for fees, we adhere strictly to your direction: *'Contact me for current details.'* No invented numbers or assumptions."

---

### [3:45 – 4:30] Mobile Experience, Seamless Booking & Crisis Safety
*(Switch browser preview to Mobile 390px viewport, demonstrating scrolling and touch interactions)*

"Now, let's look at the mobile experience. Over 60% of your prospective clients will first visit your site from their phones during a busy commute or late at night when they can't sleep.

On mobile, the navigation collapses into an elegant full-screen overlay with generous, comfortable touch targets. Notice the **sticky 'Book a Consultation' bar** that stays quietly docked at the bottom of the screen. Whether they are reading about your trauma methods or your office location, reaching out is always one effortless tap away.

When a client clicks *Book a Consultation*, it opens an accessible modal with simple, thoughtful questions: their name, email, whether they prefer Santa Monica or telehealth, and a brief note. It includes real-time validation and a calm confirmation screen once sent.

And finally, in both the mobile and desktop footers, we’ve placed a prominent, compliant crisis box with the **988 Lifeline**, ensuring clients in acute distress are directed to emergency care immediately."

---

### [4:30 – 5:00] Next Steps & Ready for Launch
*(Switch back to desktop view or full camera)*

"Everything on this website is production-ready, built with modern Next.js and TypeScript, optimized for lightning-fast load times, and structured for local Santa Monica and California SEO.

When you're ready, you can drop your final Google Drive office photos into the images folder—everything is already wired to display them seamlessly.

Take your time reviewing the draft, and I look forward to hearing your thoughts so we can launch this for your practice. Thank you, Dr. Reynolds!"
